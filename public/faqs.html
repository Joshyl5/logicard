What is Logicard?
Logicard is a discount membership card built exclusively for logistics workers, giving access to exclusive discounts across fuel, hotels, food, tech and more.
How do I join?
Sign up online in under 2 minutes, verify your eligibility, and get instant access to your membership and discounts. Create your account →
Who can apply?
Logicard is open to anyone working in logistics, including drivers, warehouse staff, fleet managers, support staff and more. See who qualifies →
How will my eligibility be verified?
Most members are verified instantly via a work email address. If you don't have one, you can upload a document instead — such as a payslip or company ID — which is reviewed within around 7 days.
What discounts can I access with Logicard?
Members get access to over 150 discounts across categories including fuel, hotels, food, tech, fleet maintenance and more.
