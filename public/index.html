FAQs

Everything you need to know before you join.

What is Logicard?

Logicard is the number 1 discount service for eligible logistics workers in the UK, including anyone involved in the production, storage, transport and stocking of goods, and the management of the processes behind the scenes. Members can access exclusive savings from thousands of brands across everyday shopping, fashion, travel, motoring, days out, home and lifestyle.

How do I join?

Register online to join Logicard. Your first year is free, then membership is just £10 per year. The FREE code is applied for you at checkout. Start your sign-up here.

Who can apply?

Logicard is available for anyone who works in, or has retired from, the logistics industry. Check if you're eligible.

How will my eligibility be verified?

There are two ways we verify your eligibility:

  • Instant email verification: if you have a recognised work email from a known logistics company, you're verified instantly when you sign up.
  • Document upload: otherwise, upload proof of employment - a work ID badge, recent payslip or official letter showing your name, employer and role. Our team typically reviews documents within 7 days.

During busy periods, processing times may vary slightly.

What discounts can I access?

Members have access to over 150 discounts with local, regional and national UK brands, online and in store, across many categories. Browse the hot deals.

How do I use my digital card?

Your card lives in your member dashboard on your phone. Show it at partner tills, or tap a deal to reveal your code for online checkouts.
