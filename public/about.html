Our Story

We've Lived The Graft Ourselves

Logicard was created by logistics workers, for logistics workers. We know this industry from the inside — because we've worked in it.

Too often, we felt overworked, underpaid, and overlooked. Long shifts, tight schedules and thankless miles are the norm across warehousing, transport, freight and supply chain roles — yet the people doing that work rarely get the recognition they've earned.

So we built the thing we wished existed: a card that says thank you in a way that actually helps.
